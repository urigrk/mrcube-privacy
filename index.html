Privacy Policy for Mr Cube

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how Mr Cube handles information. In short: we, the developers, do not collect or receive your personal information, but the game shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game does not require an account, and it does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Data stored on your device

The game saves progress and settings (such as your score, wave, skins, and preferences) locally on your device. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to revive). AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, language, and interactions with ads)
  • Diagnostic data (crash and performance logs)

Google uses this data to deliver, measure, and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. The data is encrypted in transit. To learn more, see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Your choices

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Watching ads is always optional.

Children's privacy

Mr Cube is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
