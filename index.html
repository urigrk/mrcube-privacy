Privacy Policy for Mr Cube

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Mr Cube. This policy explains how the game handles information. In short: the game does not collect, store, or share your personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game does not require an account, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Data stored on your device

The game may save progress and settings (such as your level, score, or preferences) locally on your device. This data stays on your device, is not sent to us or anyone else, and is removed when you uninstall the game.

Third-party services

The game does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
